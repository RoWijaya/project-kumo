KUMO UI REFRESH — SOURCE NOTES

This full source archive is based on the project supplied as Kumo(4).zip and includes the
UI refresh. It also retains the original assets/backend/db.php from the supplied source,
which is configured for local XAMPP (localhost/root/users). Do NOT upload that db.php over
your live InfinityFree version. For the live site, use Kumo-UI-Refresh-Patch.zip and leave
assets/backend/db.php and storage/kumo_app.key untouched.

The landing/auth background is implemented in assets/css/kumo-background.css and is fixed
to the viewport rather than document height. The background itself is deliberately static;
other UI entry and hover animations are retained.
